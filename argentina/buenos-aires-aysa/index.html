Argentina has no national water-test database. These pages include only places whose water company or regulator publishes results. Mendoza towns use AySAM’s monthly bacteria reports for six months of 2025. Buenos Aires uses ERAS figures for AySA’s whole service area, which are not published district by district.

Tap-That lists official drinking-water figures for each place. The numbers are not a live feed, and conditions can change. Follow local boil-water notices.
