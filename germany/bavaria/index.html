Safety figures are national: in 2020–2022 nearly every tested parameter was met in more than 99% of checks across 2,507 large supply zones. Hardness is the local supplier figure and varies by district. These pages cover the 150 largest cities.

Can I drink the tap water in Bavaria, Germany?

Yes. Tap water in Bavaria, Germany is low risk across 15 listed water systems. The safety figure is national, not a town-by-town test.

Where do these ratings come from?

German Federal Ministry of Health and Umweltbundesamt, drinking water report 2020–2022; hardness from supplier analyses compiled by wasser-haerte.de. Period covered: 2020 to 2022.
