Period covered: 2020 to 2022.

Germany does not publish drinking-water results town by town. Safety figures are national: in 2020–2022 nearly every tested parameter was met in more than 99% of checks across 2,507 large supply zones. Hardness is the local supplier figure and varies by district. These pages cover the 150 largest cities.

Tap-That lists official drinking-water figures for each place. The numbers are not a live feed, and conditions can change. Follow local boil-water notices.
