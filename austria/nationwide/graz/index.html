Is it safe?

✓

Graz

Low risk

Safe to drink from the tap

At large supplies, 99.9% of E. coli tests met the limit in 2025: 8 of 9,194 tests failed, at 7 of 280 supplies. Enterococci tests met the limit in 99.8% of 9,192 tests.

At smaller supplies that provide more than 100 cubic metres a day or serve more than 500 people, 99.3% of E. coli tests met the limit: 52 of 7,610 tests failed, at 36 of 1,435 supplies. Enterococci tests met the limit in 99.0% of 7,605 tests.

The report records no incident in 2025. An incident is a health risk lasting more than 10 days and affecting at least 1,000 people, and any boil order of that scale. 43 chemical derogations were in force.

Austria does not publish a town-by-town safety result, so these figures are national. Supplies below those sizes are not included.
