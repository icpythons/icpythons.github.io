Nationwide5 water systems · 5 low risk

Source

Austrian Ministry of Labour, Social Affairs, Health, Care and Consumer Protection, drinking-water report 2025.

Period covered: 2025.

Austria does not publish a result for each town. The 2025 report covers supplies that provide more than 100 cubic metres a day or serve more than 500 people. E. coli tests met the limit in 99.9% of tests at large supplies and 99.3% at smaller supplies in that range. The report records no incident in 2025. 43 chemical derogations were in force.
