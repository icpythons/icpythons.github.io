Common questions

Can I drink the tap water in Île-de-France, France?

It depends on the town. 486 of 615 listed water systems in Île-de-France, France are low risk (79%).

Where do these ratings come from?

French Ministry of Health drinking water checks (data.gouv.fr). Period covered: January to September 2026.
