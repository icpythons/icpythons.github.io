Source

Central Statistics Office and the EPA, drinking-water compliance (PxStat EIIA11).

Period covered: 2024.

Ireland does not publish drinking-water results city by city. In 2024, 99.9% of public-supply tests met the E. coli standard and 98.4% met the trihalomethane standard. Public group schemes were 100% and 98.8%. Private group schemes were 97.7% and 94.9%. The rating uses the public-supply E. coli figure. These pages list major cities with that national figure.
