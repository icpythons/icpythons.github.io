Dublin

Low risk

Safe to drink from the tap

99.9% of public-supply tests met the E. coli standard in 2024.

98.4% of public-supply tests met the trihalomethane standard in 2024.

Public group schemes: 100% met the E. coli standard and 98.8% met the trihalomethane standard. Private group schemes: 97.7% and 94.9%.

Ireland does not publish a city-by-city safety result, so these figures are national. The rating uses the public-supply E. coli figure.

Source

Ireland does not publish drinking-water results city by city. In 2024, 99.9% of public-supply tests met the E. coli standard and 98.4% met the trihalomethane standard. Public group schemes were 100% and 98.8%. Private group schemes were 97.7% and 94.9%. The rating uses the public-supply E. coli figure. These pages list major cities with that national figure.
