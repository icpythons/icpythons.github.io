How to read a rating

  • Low risk: the reported tests met the standard used for that country.
  • Medium: some tests failed, or the result is weaker.
  • High and critical: many tests failed. Check local advice.

The map inside the app also shows a national overview for countries that do not yet have town pages. Taste ratings, saved places, and “near me” stay in the app.

Tap-That lists official drinking-water figures for each place. The numbers are not a live feed, and conditions can change. Follow local boil-water notices.
