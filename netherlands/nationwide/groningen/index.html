Groningen

Low risk

Safe to drink from the tap

99.84% of measurements in the statutory programme met the standards in 2024.

Drinking-water companies issued 158 boil-water advisories in 2024. Five of them covered an area with more than 10,000 connections: Vitens issued 3, and WML and Brabant Water issued 1 each.

The Netherlands does not publish a city-by-city safety result, so this figure is national.
