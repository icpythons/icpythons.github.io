Croatia does not publish a pass rate for each city. In 2024 the public-health institutes tested 8,881 samples from the public network, 80.6% of the number planned. After allowed exceedances, 229 samples (2.6%) failed at least one parameter. Microbiological failures were 0.5% of samples, chemical failures 0.3%, and indicator failures 2.8%. A sample can be counted in more than one of those groups. These pages list major cities with that national figure.
